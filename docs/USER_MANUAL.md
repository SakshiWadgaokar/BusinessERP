# User Manual — Business ERP

## Signing In
Open the ERP portal in a browser and sign in with your registered email and password. Your navigation sidebar automatically adapts to your assigned role and permissions.

## 1. Super Admin / Owner
- **Dashboard:** Review all 19 live business KPIs, filter by Today / Yesterday / 7d / 30d / Month, inspect Sales vs. Cash Collected, Production, Dealer Performance, and Low-Stock Alerts.
- **Dealers & Orders:** Register dealers with custom credit limits and portal passwords, approve/reject orders, and dispatch goods with automatic invoicing.
- **Administration:** Manage Users (`/admin/users`), customize Role Permissions (`/admin/roles`), inspect Audit Logs (`/admin/audit-logs`), and configure GST/Costing/Credit settings (`/admin/settings`).

## 2. Accountant
- **Payments (`/finance/payments`):** Record full, partial, or advance payments, allocate across invoices, download PDF receipts, or issue audited reversals.
- **Dealer Ledger (`/finance/ledger`):** Review chronological debit/credit statements and outstanding receivables.
- **Expenses & Reports:** Log business expenses (`/finance/expenses`) and export Sales/Payment/Financial reports (`/reports`) in PDF, Excel, or CSV.

## 3. Inventory Manager
- **Catalog & Warehouses:** Manage Raw Materials, Finished Products, and Stock Locations.
- **Stock Control (`/inventory`, `/inventory/transfers`, `/purchases`):** Record Opening Stock, receive Goods Receipts (GRN) with accepted/rejected quantities, transfer stock between Warehouse and Factory, and request audited stock adjustments.

## 4. Dealer Portal (Mobile-Friendly)
- **Place Order (`/dealer/place-order`):** Order available catalog products.
- **Track & Pay:** View live order statuses (`/dealer/orders`), purchase history (`/dealer/purchases`), read-only account statement (`/dealer/ledger`), and download Invoice & Receipt PDFs (`/dealer/invoices`, `/dealer/payments`).

## 5. Employee / Factory Worker Portal (Mobile-Friendly)
- **Assigned Job Cards (`/employee/job-cards`):** View assigned jobs, log actual material consumption and defective items, and submit finished production output.
- **Material Requests & Scrap (`/employee/material-requests`, `/employee/scrap`):** Request initial or replacement materials and log scrap with auto-generated Scrap IDs.
